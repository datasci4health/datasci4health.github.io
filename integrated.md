# Integrated Studies

## Thyroid Carcinoma (THCA) case, microRNA, Pathways, and Language Models

The central problem starts from thyroid carcinoma (THCA) and goes deep into the study of the MAP kinase pathway, which is overrepresented in the differential expression and enrichment analysis. It branches in three directions:

* **mRNA-miR expression networks**: [networks/microRNA folder](networks/microRNA/)
* **MAP kinase pathway as knowledge graph**: [knowledge-graphs/pathways/mapk folder](knowledge-graphs/pathways/)
* **MAP kinase genes embeddings**: [language-model/mapk-genes-embeddings folder](language-model/mapk-genes-embeddings/)
* **GAT - Graph Attention Network integrating mRNA-miR and MAP kinase**: [language-model/gat folder](language-model/gat/)
